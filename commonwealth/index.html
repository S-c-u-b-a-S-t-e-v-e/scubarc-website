How it works

Two ways to contribute. Both voluntary. Both governed.

Commonwealth.ai is a Scuba Research Collective pilot testing whether ordinary people can safely add computing power and human problem-solving to Virginia's research infrastructure.

1

Donate Compute

Your browser runs one bounded, harmless work unit. You see the capability check, give explicit consent, and return only a constrained result receipt for server-side verification. No arbitrary file uploads, no shell access, no access to personal files.

2

Play Commonwealth Surf

A simple browser game where your decisions create candidate scheduling and recovery choices for research workloads. The server computes the authoritative score. Highest verified distance today wins $25. Free entry. Unlimited attempts.

Contest rules

Commonwealth Surf — Genesis Weekend

  • Free to play. No purchase necessary.
  • Unlimited attempts.
  • Highest verified distance today wins $25.
  • Tie: first valid score achieved.
  • Server leaderboard is authoritative.
  • ScubaRC organizer/exhibition entries are not prize eligible.

Privacy & participation

Voluntary. Minimal data. You control it.

  • No email required
  • No password required
  • No real name required
  • No phone number required
  • No precise GPS required
  • No account registration required

Nickname may be used publicly on the leaderboard. Virginia participation opt-in and optional coarse locality (city/county) are offered where supported. Participation is entirely voluntary — stop anytime by closing the page.
