SCUBARC GENESIS

COMMUNITY COMPUTE. HUMAN PARTICIPATION.

A deployed Scuba Research Collective experiment testing whether ordinary browsers and people can participate in governed research infrastructure through bounded compute and low-friction public engagement.

How it works

Two ways to participate. Both voluntary. Both governed.

ScubaRC Genesis tests whether ordinary people will voluntarily contribute bounded browser compute and human participation to a governed research-infrastructure model. The current phase measures participation, execution, verification, and engagement first. Future experiments may test scientifically legitimate public-data workloads under separate research and validation gates.

1

Contribute Compute

Your browser runs one bounded, harmless work unit. You see the capability check, give explicit consent, and return only a constrained result receipt for server-side verification. No arbitrary file uploads, no shell access, no access to personal files.

2

Play Commonwealth Surf

A simple browser game testing whether people will voluntarily participate and engage in a governed research-infrastructure pilot. The server computes the authoritative score. Compare your verified distance on the leaderboard. No cash prizes. Free entry. Unlimited attempts.

Built, tested, bounded

The prototype is live. The claims remain intentionally narrow.

Genesis uses server-authoritative results, bounded work envelopes, independent build/admission verification, adversarial timing and security testing, preview/production separation, rollback controls, real-device QA, and a formal post-mission lessons-learned process. The current compute workload is synthetic and deterministic. Genesis does not yet establish scientific usefulness, statewide scale, K–12 suitability, or improved research outcomes.

Naming note: This ScubaRC pilot was initially published under the working name Commonwealth.ai. After recognizing the similarity to the Commonwealth AI Initiative (CAII), ScubaRC adopted the name ScubaRC Genesis to avoid confusion. This pilot is independent and does not represent CAII, SURA, any university, or a Commonwealth of Virginia program.
