Physical AI, robotics & sensor-fusion research

ATHENA

Under development

Research and development connecting multi-sensor perception, operator situational awareness, and advanced robotic applications. ATHENA investigates how people and intelligent physical systems can interpret their surroundings and work together with clear human oversight.

Research objective

Reliable situational awareness when it matters most.

  • Fuse heterogeneous sensor streams into a coherent, real-time picture.
  • Support operator decision-making under stress, fatigue, and degraded communication.
  • Study reliable human-machine trust and operator-in-the-loop control.
  • Prioritize protective, non-offensive applications of physical-AI cognition.
Advanced robotic applications

From sensing a situation to supporting useful action.

ScubaRC’s robotics work investigates the relationship between machine perception, human decisions, and physical operation. ATHENA focuses on sensing, interpretation, and operator interaction, with potential applications in human–robot teaming and supervised remote systems.

Application research

Remote inspection

Explore how sensors and robotic platforms could help an operator examine difficult-to-access areas and identify where closer inspection is needed.

Application research

Environmental observation

Investigate how physical systems could gather observations while communicating sensor limitations and uncertainty to a human reviewer.

Application research

Human–robot teaming

Study operator awareness, understandable machine feedback, and human control when conditions or connectivity change.

Related project · Under development

ARES

ARES explores field computing and system integration for demanding physical-AI applications. Its relationship with ATHENA brings together the compute platform, sensor-supported awareness, and the needs of a human operator.

Advanced robotic applications are part of this development direction. Specific hardware configurations, integration methods, and unpublished designs are not included in the public overview.

Evidence to establish

Performance must be demonstrated in context.

Proposed demonstrations should measure perception accuracy, latency, communication loss and recovery, and operator workload. Human intervention and clear operating limits are central evaluation questions. This overview does not claim a deployed autonomous robot or validated safety-critical capability.

Boundary

Protective, not offensive.

ATHENA research is framed around personal and personnel protection—situational awareness and evasion support—not weaponization or offensive targeting.

Specific sensor architectures, fusion methods, and system internals remain offline pending further development and IP protection.
