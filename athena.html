Physical-AI cognition & sensor-fusion research

ATHENA

Under development

A research effort into physical-AI cognition systems that fuse multi-sensor input into real-time situational awareness for human operators in high-stress environments.

Research objective

Reliable situational awareness when it matters most.

  • Fuse heterogeneous sensor streams into a coherent, real-time picture.
  • Support operator decision-making under stress, fatigue, and degraded communication.
  • Study reliable human-machine trust and operator-in-the-loop control.
  • Prioritize protective, non-offensive applications of physical-AI cognition.
Boundary

Protective, not offensive.

ATHENA research is framed around personal and personnel protection—situational awareness and evasion support—not weaponization or offensive targeting.

Specific sensor architectures, fusion methods, and system internals remain offline pending further development and IP protection.
