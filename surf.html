How to play

Simple controls

  • ← / A — Steer left
  • → / D — Steer right
  • Space / Tap lane — Center
  • Touch: tap left/center/right thirds of screen

Avoid buoys, rocks, and debris. Crash ends your run. Distance is your score.

Research Participation

Your interaction informs public-interest research.
