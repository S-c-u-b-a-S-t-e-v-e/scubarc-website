AI compute & orchestration infrastructure research

Hermes Research Fabric

Concept validation

Research into a compute and orchestration backbone for deploying physical-AI cognition systems reliably at the edge and in research environments.

Research objective

Infrastructure that keeps up with the cognition layer.

  • Support reliable orchestration of multi-model, multi-sensor AI workloads.
  • Study the compute and connectivity needs of edge-deployed cognition systems.
  • Explore resilient architectures for degraded or contested connectivity.
  • Provide a research-grade foundation other ScubaRC projects can build on.
Boundary

Infrastructure research, not a commercial product yet.

Hermes Research Fabric is an internal research and development effort supporting ScubaRC's physical-AI work.

It is not yet offered as a standalone commercial product or service, and specific architecture and implementation details remain offline pending further development and IP protection.
